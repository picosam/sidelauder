# S3: moving a Claude window into the slot

Spike of spec §16.1 (and §9.4, the Mode B slot handoff), run 2026-09-29 on
macOS 27.0, Apple silicon, one 2560 × 1440 pt display, with
profiles A and B. Harness:
[`spikes/Sources/SpikeRunner/Frames.swift`](../../spikes/Sources/SpikeRunner/Frames.swift);
recording analysis: [`spikes/s3/`](../../spikes/s3/).

**Verdict: accuracy passes; flicker is measured but the operator's verdict
moves to M1a.** Every frame landed within 1 pt, and ordering 2 (set the frame
while the target is hidden) never showed the target outside the slot, where
ordering 1 did in 8 of 20 relocations. Both orderings show 2 to 4 in-between
frames when the window changes size. Whether that is visible at normal
speed, the operator will judge in the built app, with the real rail.

## Method

Each switch reproduced a rail click. SpikeRunner (Accessibility granted, as
Mode B requires) clicked its own panel, became active, handed the slot to
the hidden target, activated it with step 1, and hid the source.

- **Ordering 1:** unhide the target, find its main window, set size,
  position, size (Appendix A.3), activate, hide the source.
- **Ordering 2:** set the frame on the still-hidden target, then unhide,
  activate, hide the source.

Each ordering ran 30 plain switches and 20 relocated ones. In the plain
switches both windows sit in the slot after the first switch, so almost
nothing moves. In the relocated switches the hidden target was first moved
and resized somewhere else, so every switch has to bring it back. The
operator recorded the screen throughout.

The recording (4096 × 2304, variable frame rate, about 56 fps) was sampled
at 60 Hz around every switch and two kinds of frame were counted:

- **outside:** the screen outside the slot differs from both its state
  before the click and after the switch (a window shown where it should not
  be);
- **inside:** the slot differs from both before and after, showing neither
  the source nor the target as it ends up.

A positive control required the slot to change across every switch. It did,
in 100 of 100, once the analysis was aligned on the logged switches. The
runner's pink start marker reached the recording in one frame at most and
could not anchor it.

## Results

| Ordering | Relocated | Switches | Frame within 1 pt | Target activated, source hidden | Switch time p50 / p95 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | no | 30 | 30/30 | 30/30 | 50 / 63 ms |
| 1 | yes | 20 | 20/20 | 20/20 | 65 / 79 ms |
| 2 | no | 30 | 30/30 | 30/30 | 50 / 69 ms |
| 2 | yes | 20 | 20/20 | 20/20 | 63 / 72 ms |

Switch time runs from the click to the source being hidden.

| Ordering | Relocated | Switches with any outside frame | Outside frames p50 / max | Inside frames p50 / max | Switches with more than 1 inside frame |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | no | 1 of 30 | 0 / 1 | 1 / 2 | 1 |
| 1 | yes | **8 of 20** | 0 / 2 | 2 / 4 | 11 |
| 2 | no | 0 of 30 | 0 / 0 | 1 / 2 | 1 |
| 2 | yes | **0 of 20** | 0 / 0 | 2 / 4 | 10 |

At 60 Hz one frame is about 17 ms. One inside frame is the flip itself;
more than one means in-between states, most likely Claude redrawing at the
new size.

Other facts measured:

- AX reached the main window of a **hidden** instance in 50 of 50 switches
  (ordering 2), and a frame set there held after unhiding.
- Claude's minimum window size: asked for 320 × 240, the window stopped at
  **600 × 400** pt.
- `AXEnhancedUserInterface` was off on both instances before and after
  (D-18's wrapper had nothing to do).
- The original frames and hidden states were restored afterwards.

## What this does not cover

- The operator's own flicker verdict (moved to M1a, see above).
- Two displays, Spaces, full screen and Stage Manager: S9, not in this M0
  run.
- A slot smaller than Claude's minimum: handled by adopting the actual
  frame (§9.4), verified only as the 600 × 400 clamp above.

## Design consequences

- §9.4: adopt ordering 2 (set the frame while the target is hidden, then
  unhide and activate). It removes the visible jump at no cost in time.
- §9.4's "first standard window within 800 ms" wait is not needed on a
  hidden, already-running instance; it remains for launch-into-slot (S6).
- The slot's minimum is 600 × 400 pt. §9.9's clamping re-centre must not go
  below it.
- M1a exit gains the operator's flicker judgment on the real rail.
  If it fails, the crossfade overlay (backlog) comes forward.
